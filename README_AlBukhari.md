# Asl El-Mazaq — Digital Launch Campaign for Al-Bukhari Grills

**DEPI (Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative) — Round 5 | Track: Digital Marketing & Art | Group ONL5_DRT1_G2 — Team 5 | Graduation Project**

A 360° digital marketing launch for **Al-Bukhari Grills**, a new full-menu grill restaurant in Zagazig, Egypt (soft opening: 15 October 2026).
The project covers brand identity (logo, colours, menu), an integrated marketing strategy, content production, paid Meta advertising, a launch campaign, and a review and reputation system.
